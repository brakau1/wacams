## 1. Attendance Rules

### AC-ATT-001: Clock-In Late Detection

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Module** | Attendance Calculation |
| **Priority** | P0 — Critical |
| **Test Data** | `EmployeeFixtures.GetCleanOperator()` + `AttendanceFixtures.CreateEdgeCaseRecords()` |

**Given** an employee is scheduled for Morning shift (06:00–14:00)  
**When** they clock in at 06:15  
**Then** the system marks them as Late with 15 minutes late  
**And** applies 10-minute grace period (net 5 minutes late)  
**And** creates an `AttendanceException` with `ExceptionType = LateArrival` and `MinutesLate = 5`

**Acceptance Checks:**
- [ ] `IsLate = true` on the attendance record
- [ ] `MinutesLate = 5` (after grace period deduction)
- [ ] Status = `"Late"`
- [ ] Exception created with investigation status `"Open"`

|-------|-------|

| **Priority** | P0 — Critical |

- [ ] Audit log entry created for auto-escalation trigger
